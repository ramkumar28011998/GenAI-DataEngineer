Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting
===================================
Definition:
Chain-of-Thought prompting is a technique where the model is encouraged to explain its reasoning step-by-step before giving the final answer.
Instead of jumping directly to the answer, the model breaks the problem into intermediate reasoning steps.

Why it is used?
====================
Some problems require logical reasoning, such as:
Math problems
Logical puzzles
Multi-step reasoning
Complex decision making

Chain-of-Thought helps the model think through the problem step by step, which usually improves accuracy.

Example:

Prompt:
Q: If John has 3 apples and buys 2 more, how many apples does he have?
Let's think step by step.
Output:
John starts with 3 apples.
He buys 2 more apples.
3 + 2 = 5.
Answer: 5


Instead of: Question -> Answer
CoT encourages: Question → Reasoning Steps → Final Answer

Chain-of-Thought prompting tells the model to "think step-by-step before answering."



Meta Prompting
=======================
Definition:
Meta prompting is a technique where the prompt guides the model on 
how to think or structure its response rather than directly asking for the final answer.

In simple terms:

You give instructions about the reasoning strategy the model should follow.
It is like telling the model how to approach the problem.

Simple Idea
===============
Normal prompt: Explain photosynthesis.
Meta prompt: Explain photosynthesis step by step as if teaching a beginner.

Here you are guiding the thinking style of the model.

What Meta Prompting Usually Specifies
============================================
You may instruct the model to:

Think step-by-step
Act as an expert
Follow a structured format
Critically review its answer
Break the task into subtasks

Meta prompting = prompting the model about how to reason, not just what to answer.

Difference from Chain-of-Thought
================================
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Technique        | Idea                                                       |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chain-of-Thought | Ask the model to show reasoning steps                      |
| Meta Prompting   | Tell the model **how to think or structure the reasoning** |
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
